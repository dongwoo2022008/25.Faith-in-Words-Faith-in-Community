## C. Highlights 초안 (각 85자 이내)
1. Borrowers employed by religious institutions default less (odds ratio 0.69) [75]
2. Borrowers who invoke faith in their loan request default more, not less [71]
3. The affiliation advantage holds for church secretaries as well as clergy [72]
4. The advantage grows where congregations are denser, within and across ZIP areas [79]
5. Neither signal is reflected in LendingClub's grade-based interest rates [71]
