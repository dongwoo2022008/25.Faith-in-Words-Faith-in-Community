## 4. Diagnosis and what changed

1. **First finding with magnitude:** it already comes earlier than in every benchmark (¶2). No change.
2. **Contributions diffuse:** ¶7 ("three things") repeated the end of ¶5, and ¶5 combined two literatures in about 390 words. All five benchmarks that list contributions give one block per literature. v10 splits ¶5 into two literature paragraphs, deletes the ¶7 repeat, and moves ¶7's only new point (community, not vocation, grows with density) to the end of literature 1.
3. **Positioning sentences in the claim paragraph:** the soft-information literature sentences (Freedman and Jin; Duarte et al.; Lin et al.; Davaadorj et al.) sat at the end of ¶3, between the claim and "That last set of facts". In v10 they move, unchanged, into the literature-3 paragraph, and ¶4 opens with an explicit referent.
4. **Limits placement:** in 4 of 5 benchmarks, caveats and robustness come before the contribution paragraphs. v10 moves the identification/limits paragraph ahead of the literatures.
5. **Pricing and data missing from the intro:** 4 of 5 benchmarks report the market-pricing result in the intro, and all 5 name their data. v10 adds both in ¶2, without new digits.
6. **Roadmap:** it now matches the order of §5 (Prosper sits in §5.1, and timing is §5.6) and includes §6.3.
7. **Conclusion restates only:** 4 of 5 benchmarks go beyond restatement to implications. v10 adds one implications paragraph and keeps the closing sentence.
